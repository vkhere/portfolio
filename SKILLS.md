## 8. Skill Representation Logic

Progress bar widths are calculated as follows:

```
EMC Avamar  → 4.7 years  → (4.7 / 5) × 100 ≈ 94%
Commvault   → 3.3 years  → (3.3 / 4) × 100 ≈ 82%
Rubrik      → 4.11 years → capped at 97% (near-expert)
NetApp etc. → 9 months   → 65-70% (actively learning)
```

This ensures honest, proportional representation of experience depth.
